> "Here's the single biggest mistake people make when vibe coding — and it costs them HOURS.
>
> They open one AI tool, type 'build me a website,' and expect magic. No requirements. No design system. No plan. Just vibes.
>
> Here's the fix. Before you write ANY code, spend 10 minutes doing this:
>
> One — write a requirements doc. What pages, what features, what style.
