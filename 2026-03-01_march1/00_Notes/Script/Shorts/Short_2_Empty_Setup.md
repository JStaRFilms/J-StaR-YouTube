# Short 2 - Empty Setup

## Angle
The AI is not the problem, the setup is empty

## Script
I used to think the AI was the problem.

But the real problem was that my workspace was empty.

No context files. No coding standards. No design system. No checks.

So every time I asked it to build something, it had to guess.

And guessed output might look fine at first.

But once the project gets real, it starts falling apart.

That was the shift for me.

I stopped blaming the AI and started fixing the setup.

Once I gave it a real system to work from, the results got way better.

That's part of what I break down in the full video.
