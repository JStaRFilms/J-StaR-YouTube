# Short 3 - Demo vs Real App

## Angle
What AI misses after the first result

## Script
Anybody can get AI to make something that looks good for five minutes.

I've done that too.

Nice layout. Clean screen. Maybe even good animations.

But that's not the hard part.

The hard part is what AI usually misses once the project gets real.

Security gaps.

Broken logic.

Missing checks.

Stuff that looks fine at first, then causes problems later.

That was the shift for me.

I stopped judging AI by the first result and started judging it by what happens after.

That's part of what I break down in the full video.
